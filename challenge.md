## Challenge Guidelines

Both phases of the NLB'21 Challenge have ended. Phase 1 of the challenge ended on 1/7/2022, won by [AE Studio](https://ae.studio/). Phase 2 of the challenge ended on 4/3/2022 and had multiple winners, including [STNDT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04727) from Trung Le and the Shlizerman lab and [RNN_F](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06040) from Ganga Meghanath and the Makin lab. Since Phase 2, a number of interesting and competitive models have been submitted to the benchmark, including [S5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04933) from Jimmy Smith and the Linderman lab and [Langevin Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11531) from Yue Song and the Perona Lab. You can view the full leaderboard as of January 2026 on [EvalAI](https://eval.ai/web/challenges/challenge-page/1256/leaderboard).

Though the EvalAI challenge is no longer accepting submissions, you can still evaluate your methods on the now-public test data available in the [repo](https://github.com/neurallatents/nlb_tools) for our codepack.
